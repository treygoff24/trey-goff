## Epistemic frame (read first)

Every person in this cluster is a **single first-person witness** whose entire evidentiary weight is *their own say-so*. The Disclosure Project transcripts (Greer, National Press Club, 9 May 2001) are the **primary source for what was *claimed*** — solid **type-A** evidence ("person X said Y"). They are **not** evidence that the claims are *true* (**type-C**). Not one of these accounts is backed by a surviving document, a photograph, a corroborating named co-worker, or a second independent witness to the same event. This distinction is held throughout below.

**Disambiguation the ledger requires:**
- **Gary McKinnon** = the British *hacker* (Section H, C122–C127). The ledger names him correctly; his claims match the hacker's record exactly. He is **not** Karl Wolfe.
- **Karl Wolfe** = a *separate* Disclosure Project witness (USAF photo technician, "base on the far side of the Moon"). **Wolfe is NOT actually referenced in Section H** — the ledger contains no Wolfe claim. He is the canonical fourth member of this "photo-scrubbing / hidden-imagery whistleblower" cluster, so he is worked up below as adjacent context and to pre-empt the Wolfe/McKinnon confusion the task flagged.
- **Takanobu Jausen / Takano Jyusen (sp?)** = **Jōmei / Jōsen Takano** (高野), founder-figure of the **Cosmo Isle Hakui** space museum in Ishikawa, Japan. This is the Japan figure of C091–C093 and C128–C131, and the "Jausen" of C094–C095.

## 1. Donna Hare (C125, C126)

**Documented background.** Hare worked for NASA contractor **Philco Ford (Ford Aerospace)** from **1967 to 1981** as a **design illustrator / draftsman** (launch slides, landing slides, plotting-board lunar maps). She was based mostly in **Building 8** at Johnson Space Center and held a **Secret** clearance — which, in her own words, "is not that high but I was able to go into restricted areas." This much (contractor illustrator, Building 8, Secret clearance) is internally consistent and is the strongest-documented biographical part of the cluster. She died in 2019/2020; she is deceased as of the podcast.

**What she claimed, verbatim** (Disclosure Project testimony, as transcribed at thenightskyii.org/hare.html; the substance matches the video of her 2001 National Press Club appearance):

> "Good morning everyone, my name is Donna Hare, and I worked at Philco Ford aerospace from 1967 to 1981. During that time, I was a design illustrator draftsman... I walked into a photo lab which was the NASA lab across the hallway. I had a secret clearance which is not that high but I was able to go into restricted areas. At the time, I was talking to one of the techs in there and he drew my attention to a NASA photograph. It had a dot on it, and I asked what it was... I said is that a dot on the emulsion? He was smiling and he had his hands crossed. This was an aerial photograph of the Earth... the shadows of the craft or whatever it was were at the same angle as the trees and by its very nature, a UFO... I asked him what he was gonna do with this piece of information. **He said they always airbrush these out before they sell them to the public**..."

This is the source of the ledger's C126 quote ("We always airbrush these out before we sell them to the public"). The ledger's wording is a close paraphrase; the archival transcript reads "they always airbrush these out before they sell them to the public."

**Firsthand vs. secondhand (critical).** The *only* firsthand element is: she says she was shown a photo with a white dot, and a technician made the airbrush remark to her. **She did not see anyone airbrush anything, did not keep or photograph the image, and cannot name the technician.** Everything else in her testimony is explicitly secondhand and hearsay-of-hearsay:
- A *guard* told her he was asked to burn photographs (and peeked at one).
- Someone "in quarantine with the Apollo astronauts" told her astronauts "saw crap on the Moon."
- A source told her "3 UFOs had landed" during a Moon landing and that the codeword was "Santa Claus" (this feeds the ledger's C158 "Santa Claus" code-name claim).

**Corroboration status: none that is independent.** No original photo, no named technician, no documented "airbrush" policy has ever been produced. The only "corroboration" offered by supporters is *other uncorroborated whistleblower testimony* (Wolfe; a "Marian Rudynk"), i.e. more of the same citation circle, not physical evidence.

**Best skeptical rebuttal (James Oberg, veteran NASA/spaceflight skeptic), verbatim:**

> "her key observation — seeing a photo of a hovering UFO in an earth surface photo being sanitized for commercial sale — is impossible to believe because she claims the shadow of the tree told her how high the UFO had to have been... There aren't any NASA photos released or sold in the 1970s that were sharp enough to see individual trees and their shadows, their 'ground resolution' wasn't anywhere near good enough... Without the shadow, the scale of the 'UFO' is imaginary, and the entire scenario is delusional."

Oberg also asserts Hare was "a known UFO contactee... and anything co-workers told her could easily have been mockery or pranks" — a claim about *her* that is itself an assertion, but the resolution argument is a concrete, physical objection that does not depend on impugning her.

**Steelman (anomalous side).** She had genuine access to a real photo-processing environment; retouching/dodging of imagery for publication was a real practice; and her account has stayed consistent for 20+ years. Steelman (mundane side): a downtime visitor mis-hears/mis-reads a joking tech pointing at an emulsion defect, and a UFO-contactee worldview supplies the interpretation; the resolution objection makes the "trees + shadow scale" detail physically untenable for 1970s NASA imagery.

**Provisional verdict: unsupported (leaning debunked on the specific "airbrush" mechanism).** Type-A (she said it) is confirmed. Type-C (NASA systematically airbrushes UFOs out of photos) rests on a single uncorroborated, physically-problematic anecdote. *What would change it:* the actual photograph, the named technician, or a documented retouch-for-UFO policy.

## 2. Karl Wolfe — adjacent context, NOT in Section H (disambiguation)

**Why he is here:** the task flagged that "McKinnon" could be a garble of Wolfe. It is not — Section H's McKinnon is the hacker. But Wolfe is the other classic "hidden lunar imagery" Disclosure witness and belongs in the cluster map. **No claim ID corresponds to Wolfe.**

**Documented service record.** **Karl Wolfe**, USAF **Sergeant**, served roughly **1964–1968** as a **precision electronics / photographic-repair technician**. His Disclosure account places the key event in **1965** at a facility he associates with **Langley AFB (Virginia)** — note: the archival transcript page mislabels the location as "Langley Air Force, France," which is an error (Langley AFB is in Hampton, Virginia; the NSA is headquartered at Fort Meade, Maryland). He said he was pulled in to fix computerized contact-printing equipment at an **NSA** facility handling **Lunar Orbiter** imagery, and initially misheard "NSA" as "NASA."

**What he claimed, verbatim** (thenightskyii.org/kw.html, tracking his 2001 Disclosure testimony):

> "The airman explained: **We discovered, a base on the back side of the Moon.**... Seeing Wolfe's disbelief, the dark room attendant continued: **Yes, a base on the dark side of the Moon.**... He could clearly make out geometric shapes, well organized, and well designed. Most noticeable to him were what looked exactly like radar antennas..."

**Firsthand vs. secondhand.** He claims to have *firsthand seen* photographic prints showing structures, but the interpretation ("a base," "intelligent beings") was **told to him** by an unnamed darkroom airman. No print left the room; nothing was retained.

**Corroboration:** none independent. Same single-witness, no-document structure as Hare.

**2018 death (accurate, and note the conspiracy framing):** Wolfe was **74** when he was **killed on 10 October 2018** in **Lansing, New York**, struck by a tractor-trailer while riding his **bicycle** (reported by the Ithaca Journal; picked up by Fox News). It was an ordinary road-traffic fatality; UFO sites frame it ominously ("Whistleblower Hit By Truck"), but there is no evidence of foul play.

**Provisional verdict: unsupported.** Type-A confirmed (he consistently said it for ~30 years after and gave sworn/on-camera testimony). Type-C unsupported — single uncorroborated account, no imagery.

## 3. Gary McKinnon (C122, C123, C124, C127; motivation for C125)

**The legal record (well documented, type-B solid).** McKinnon, a Scottish systems administrator in North London, accessed **97 U.S. government computers** (Army, Navy, Air Force, DoD, NASA) between **Feb 2001 and Mar 2002**, using scripted scans for blank/default admin passwords. U.S. prosecutors alleged **~$700,000** in damage and called it (at the time) among the largest military-network intrusions. He was arrested by the UK National Hi-Tech Crime Unit in **March 2002**. A **decade-long extradition fight** followed; in **October 2012** Home Secretary Theresa May **blocked extradition** on human-rights/health grounds (McKinnon has Asperger's and documented suicide risk), and the CPS declined to prosecute him in the UK in **December 2012**. That the intrusions happened is **substantiated and admitted**. That he *targeted Building 8 because of Donna Hare* (C125) is McKinnon's own stated motivation, consistent with his interviews.

**What he claimed to have found, verbatim** (Wired, "'UFO Hacker' Tells What He Found," 21 Jun 2006 — an exclusive phone interview, the primary source for the exact wording):

> "A NASA photographic expert said that there was a **Building 8 at Johnson Space Center where they regularly airbrushed out images of UFOs** from the high-resolution satellite imaging. I logged on to NASA and was able to access this department... I was able to briefly see one of these pictures. It was a **silvery, cigar-shaped object with geodesic spheres on either side**. There were no visible seams or riveting...
> I also got access to Excel spreadsheets. One was titled **'Non-Terrestrial Officers.'** It contained **names and ranks of U.S. Air Force personnel who are not registered anywhere else. It also contained information about ship-to-ship transfers**, but I've never seen the names of these ships noted anywhere else."

**Note on the ledger's wording.** The podcast/ledger says "**fleet-to-fleet** transfers"; McKinnon's actual words are "**ship-to-ship** transfers." The ledger also calls the object a "Tic Tac" (C127); McKinnon described a **cigar shape with geodesic spheres**, not a Tic Tac. Both are drift from the primary source. Note too that his stated inspiration was explicitly *the Disclosure Project book itself* ("400 testimonials") — i.e., McKinnon's UFO beliefs are downstream of the very Hare/Wolfe testimony this finding examines. The chain loops.

**What was ever substantiated:** the **hacking** (admitted). The **UFO findings** — the airbrush department, the cigar craft, the "Non-Terrestrial Officers" spreadsheet — were **never independently verified, never produced in evidence, and rest solely on McKinnon's recollection.** He himself conceded to Wired the spreadsheet "could be a game — it's hard to know for certain," and that he only ever got a fleeting low-color-depth screen view he could not save. No file, screenshot, or copy survived seizure.

**Steelman (anomalous):** a curious admin genuinely saw *something* labeled oddly on a real government network. **Steelman (mundane):** "Non-Terrestrial Officers" is entirely consistent with routine military space-operations nomenclature or a wargame/simulation dataset; the "craft" was a fleeting, 4-bit-color, unscaled screen glimpse with no provenance; and his prior UFO conviction (from the Disclosure book) primed the interpretation.

**Provisional verdict: hacking = confirmed; UFO discoveries = unsupported.** Type-A confirmed (he consistently said it). Type-C unsupported. *What would change it:* production of the actual "Non-Terrestrial Officers" file or the cigar-craft image with verifiable NASA provenance — never surfaced in 20+ years, including through a heavily litigated extradition where the defense had every incentive to substantiate it.

## 4. "Takanobu Jausen / Takano Jyusen" = Jōmei/Jōsen Takano, Cosmo Isle Hakui (C091–C093, C128–C131; C094–C095)

**Who he actually is.** The garbled name resolves to **Takano (高野)** — variously rendered Jōmei / Jōsen Takano — the driving figure behind **Cosmo Isle Hakui (コスモアイル羽咋)**, a space-science museum in **Hakui City, Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan**. Takano was a former broadcast producer / municipal official who spearheaded the museum as a town-revitalization project built around Hakui's local UFO folklore. (Note: sources also render his given name inconsistently — "Jōmei," "Jōsen," and 和尚/"oshō" appears in some Japanese blog titles, suggesting a Buddhist-priest association; the museum-founder identity is the stable, verifiable anchor.)

**The museum & the NASA-hardware claims (C092, C093) — largely real.** Cosmo Isle Hakui genuinely holds **authentic space hardware**: a Vostok/Soyuz-type Russian capsule, a Mercury-Redstone/Apollo-related items, a Lunar Module replica, and — notably — **an actual flight garment worn by Apollo 17 commander Eugene Cernan**. Takano is documented as having secured genuine NASA and Russian materials through direct negotiation with NASA and Russian space officials. So the ledger's "full NASA museum, Russian rocketry on display" (C092/C093) is **substantially TRUE**; the "100-year loan" specific framing is plausible museum-loan language but I did not verify the exact term against a primary loan document.

**The Cernan connection (C094) — real but thin.** Takano is repeatedly described as having **known Cernan personally** (consistent with the museum holding Cernan's flight garment and Cernan-related memorabilia). A genuine acquaintance/collector relationship is well-supported. Cernan did visit/associate with Japanese space-memorabilia circles.

**The extraordinary claim (C095, C097, C098) — uncorroborated, single-source hearsay.** Takano alleges that Cernan **privately told him** the Apollo 17 crew received a **telepathic message** en route to the Moon: "We don't really want you here" / "I was told never to come back" / "it came directly to my brain." This is:
- **Secondhand** (Takano relaying a private remark),
- **Never stated by Cernan on the record** (Cernan, who died in Jan 2017 and was publicly a *skeptic* who said he saw nothing anomalous, never made any such statement publicly), and
- **Uncorroborated** by any NASA record, transcript, or second witness.

**C128–C131 (Takano "at JSC, shown UFO photos/video," "I found the UFO footage," "at Johnson Space Center in Houston").** These are Michels' podcast clips of Takano. They are **type-A** (Takano said it). There is **no independent confirmation** that Takano was shown UFO footage at JSC; this is a fresh, single-source claim with the same structure as the rest of the cluster.

**Provisional verdict:** museum/hardware/Cernan-acquaintance = **likely true** (C092/C093 substantiated; C094 well-supported). The **telepathic-message and JSC-UFO-footage claims = unsupported** — single-source, secondhand, contradicted by Cernan's own public skepticism. *What would change it:* a recorded or written statement from Cernan himself, which does not exist.

That is a **citation circle**, not independent confirmation. Under the charter, mutual citation among uncorroborated testimonies does not raise type-C confidence.
